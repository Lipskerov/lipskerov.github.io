Experience

PhD Researcher — Molecular Biology / Biochemistry

Technion – Israel Institute of Technology, Faculty of Medicine · Ciechanover Lab
2022 – 2026

Thesis: Precise Mapping of the Proteasome Interaction Region of p62/SQSTM1 — Decoupling Condensate Formation from Proteasome Recruitment. Supervised by Prof. Aaron Ciechanover (Nobel Laureate in Chemistry, 2004).

  • Engineered systematic deletion and point-mutant panels of p62/SQSTM1 to map the Proteasome Interaction Region (PIR) down to a six-residue stretch within the PB1 domain
  • Showed that losing the PIR selectively disrupts proteasome recruitment into p62 condensates while leaving liquid–liquid phase separation (LLPS) intact — separating two functions previously bundled together
  • Confocal and super-resolution fluorescence microscopy: live-cell and fixed-sample imaging, condensate dynamics, quantitative image analysis
  • Co-immunoprecipitation, cycloheximide-chase degradation assays, and in-cell proteasome activity assays
  • Structural modeling, homology and docking analyses used to guide and rationalize the mutagenesis design
  • Published as first author in Cells (2026) — doi.org/10.3390/cells15040335
